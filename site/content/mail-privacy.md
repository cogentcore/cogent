Title = "Cogent Mail privacy policy"
+++

**Cogent Mail** is a free and open source email app, made by the Cogent Core project, that runs on your own computer. This policy describes what it does with your data, including the data of your Google account.

## What the app accesses

When you add a Gmail account, you sign in with Google in your browser and allow Cogent Mail to access your mail (the `https://mail.google.com/` scope), plus your email address and name (the `openid`, `email`, and `profile` scopes). The app uses this access only to provide the features of an email client that you use: downloading, showing, searching, sending, filing, labeling, and deleting your messages, with the IMAP and SMTP servers of Gmail.

## Where your data is kept

Everything stays on your computer. Cogent Mail keeps a copy of your messages, an index of them for searching, your settings, and the sign-in token from Google in the app data folder of your user account on your computer. The app talks only to the mail servers of your accounts and to Google for signing in.

Cogent Mail has no servers of its own, and the Cogent Core project does not receive, collect, store, or see any of your data. Nothing is sold, shared with others, used for advertising, or used to train AI models. The app has no analytics or tracking.

## Google user data

Cogent Mail's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the [Google API Services User Data Policy](https://developers.google.com/terms/api-services-user-data-policy), including the Limited Use requirements.

## Removing your data

Removing an account in Cogent Mail (Remove account in the context menu of the account) deletes its local copy of your mail and its sign-in token from your computer; your mail stays in your account. You can also revoke the access of the app at any time on the [third-party connections page](https://myaccount.google.com/connections) of your Google account. Deleting the app data folder of Cogent Mail deletes all of its data.

## Changes and contact

Changes to this policy are made on this page, and are in the history of the [source code of the website](https://github.com/cogentcore/cogent). For questions, open an issue at [github.com/cogentcore/cogent](https://github.com/cogentcore/cogent/issues).
